Anex 3D is a 3D printing company that supplies 3D printers and filaments, alongside offering custom 3D printing services.
Brief. The goal of this project was to design a logo and logomark for Anex 3D’s website, making slight references to the 3D print industry and using orange as the main colour, alongside product photography to advertise the filaments being sold. Response. The Anex logo took heavy inspiration from the perceived industrial, futuristic elements of 3D printing, with a strong geometric typeface, with the letters pushed in together to create a solid object that references the infill of a 3D printer. The ‘A’ is half solid and half empty as a symbolic way of showing a 3D print half printed, which creates a strong logo mark that reflects the print process.

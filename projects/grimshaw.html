Grimshaw Band is a punk metal band based in Melbourne.
Brief. Grimshaw Band hires me as their photographer for their live performances, and commissioned me to produce a ten-second animation for Instagram to promote their new demo, ‘Black eyes.’ Response. For their live performances, a custom style has been developed to reflect the band. Soft tones of blue sit in the shadows, and orange in the highlights, reflecting the band’s colours paired with a high grain image to make the tone feel dark and grungy, representing the sound of the band in a visual form. For ‘Black Eye’s’ animation, I took inspiration from the UK’s punk rock scene of the 1970s, with the premise focused on the aftermath of a bar fight. Produced with handmade paper assets photographed to create a random sense of movement that has a sense of physicality and texture to reference the independent, handmade production of the punk scene.
